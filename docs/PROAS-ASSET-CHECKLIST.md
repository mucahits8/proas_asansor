# PROAS Asset Checklist

Bu liste, mevcut siteyi demo/placeholder seviyesinden gerçek kurumsal kanıt seviyesine taşımak için gereken materyalleri toplar.

## Company

- High resolution logo files
- Official company profile text
- Confirmed foundation or activity year
- Current office addresses
- Current service areas
- Approved short company description

## Projects

- 5 priority projects
- Building or project name
- City
- Year
- Number of systems
- Elevator type
- Scope of work
- 5-10 photos per project if possible

## System Images

- Passenger elevator
- Panoramic elevator
- Patient or stretcher elevator
- Freight elevator
- Car elevator
- Accessibility platform or stairlift

## Field

- Installation team
- Maintenance team
- Technician portrait or action photos
- Control panel
- Machine room
- Shaft or cabin detail photos

## Corporate

- Team photos
- Office photos
- Founder or management photos if desired
- Historic photos
- Regional operation photos

## Production

- Factory or workshop
- Warehouse
- Assembly area
- Cabin or door manufacturing
- Metal work
- Quality control area

## Documents

- Current certificates
- Certificate PDFs
- Validity dates
- Service qualification documents
- Quality standard documents

## References

- Confirmed reference list
- Logo permissions
- Logo assets
- Publicly shareable project names
- Approved system counts
